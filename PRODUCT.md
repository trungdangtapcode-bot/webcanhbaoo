# Product

## Register

product

## Users
City operators, dispatchers, and citizens monitoring real-time urban incidents. They need quick, actionable situational awareness of traffic jams, fires, floods, and camera feeds within a specific geographic radius.

## Product Purpose
A live operations "Command Center" dashboard and map. It provides immediate spatial context and alerting for urban emergencies and traffic conditions, allowing users to filter, locate, and assess situations rapidly.

## Brand Personality
Professional, urgent, high-tech, precise.

## Anti-references
- Do NOT use SaaS-cream or warm/parchment backgrounds; this is a tactical dashboard, not a lifestyle blog.
- Avoid overly rounded playful UI elements (no 24px+ border radii on cards).
- Avoid cartoonish or sketchy illustrations.
- No generic template metrics (hero-metric template).

## Design Principles
- **Data density without clutter:** Maximize the map visibility while keeping critical controls accessible.
- **Expert confidence:** Interactions should feel responsive, deterministic, and built for power users.
- **Focus on geography:** The map is the primary canvas; all other UI elements are secondary overlays.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for critical alerts (e.g., distinct red for fires, blue for floods).
- Legible typography over map backgrounds (using proper glassmorphism or solid backdrops).
- Interface must remain usable in varied lighting conditions (e.g., dark mode optimization).
